Your music, made meat.

Wurst is a macOS app that takes the audio coming out of your Mac — an album, a film, a call you probably shouldn't be visualising — and renders it as a single sausage. Bass fills the belly. Treble crisps the ends. The casing browns as the track gets louder and splits when it gets too loud.

Leave it up on a second screen for the length of a record, or export the whole thing as video.

See the casings

Eight casings.

Each one changes the colour, the grain of the mince and how hard the thing chars under a heavy low end. Pick one and the sausage above changes.
